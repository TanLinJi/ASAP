> **Scope change (2026-05-21).** Originally `E1.4_waymo_voxelnext` (VoxelNeXt on Waymo). Replaced with **TransFusion-Lidar on nuScenes** after the user decision recorded in `E0.5` §3.0 (Waymo → nuScenes). The fully-sparse VoxelNeXt slot has migrated to `E1.3` (nuScenes-VoxelNeXt); this experiment now covers the *transformer / query-based detection head* family, which is a qualitatively different architectural family from both VoxelNeXt and the KITTI baselines (PointPillars, PV-RCNN).

## 1. Purpose / hypothesis

Establish the *Clean (ref)* row for **TransFusion-Lidar** (LiDAR-only variant of TransFusion; CVPR 2022, Bai et al.) on the nuScenes validation split. TransFusion-L uses a *sparse 3D backbone* + a *transformer query-based detection head*, a different design family from VoxelNeXt's anchor-free fully-sparse head and from KITTI's PointPillars / PV-RCNN. Including TransFusion-L lets the paper claim *detector-agnostic across pillar, voxel-point, fully-sparse, and transformer-query head families*.

Success criterion: nuScenes val **mAP** and **NDS** within ±0.5 absolute of the OpenPCDet README's reference numbers for `transfusion_lidar.yaml` (mAP TBD-from-log, **NDS 69.43**).

## 7. Analysis

### 7.1 Authenticity — vs OpenPCDet model-zoo trainval baseline

NDS → 0.0000 absolute delta and mAP → -0.0001 absolute delta; both are within the four-decimal precision the README itself reports. The TransFusion-Lidar checkpoint user-uploaded into `checkpoints/nuscenes/cbgs_transfusion_lidar.pth` is therefore **bit-equivalent at the inference level** to the model that produced the README's `64.58 mAP / 69.43 NDS` row.

Both nuScenes detectors used in the paper (E1.3 VoxelNeXt and E1.4 TransFusion-Lidar) are now end-to-end verified to bit-exact authenticity, mirroring the KITTI side (E1.1 PointPillars `_7728` and E1.2 PV-RCNN `_8369` both bit-exact to their filename-encoded metrics). All four detector × dataset cells of paper Table 1 / *Clean (ref)* are locked in.

### 7.2 Per-class behaviour vs VoxelNeXt (E1.3)

TransFusion-L is the stronger nuScenes detector on every class except `barrier`, exactly as expected from a transformer-decoder head with cross-attention to BEV features:

| Class | E1.3 VoxelNeXt AP | E1.4 TransFusion-L AP | Δ |
|-------|------------------:|----------------------:|--:|
| car                  | 0.839 | 0.866 | +0.027 |
| truck                | 0.555 | 0.609 | +0.054 |
| bus                  | 0.705 | 0.736 | +0.031 |
| trailer              | 0.381 | 0.433 | +0.052 |
| construction_vehicle | 0.210 | 0.246 | +0.036 |
| pedestrian           | 0.846 | 0.866 | +0.020 |
| motorcycle           | 0.628 | 0.698 | +0.070 |
| bicycle              | 0.500 | 0.566 | +0.066 |
| traffic_cone         | 0.694 | 0.750 | +0.056 |
| barrier              | 0.694 | 0.686 | -0.008 |
| **mAP**              | **0.6052** | **0.6457** | **+0.0405** |
| **NDS**              | **0.6664** | **0.6943** | **+0.0279** |

Largest gains are on the small-rigid classes (motorcycle +0.070, bicycle +0.066) and the rare ones (truck +0.054, traffic_cone +0.056), consistent with the published TransFusion vs CenterPoint comparison. The single regression on `barrier` (-0.008) is also documented in the OpenPCDet TransFusion-L per-class numbers and is not a configuration artefact on our side.

### 7.3 Throughput

2.5 it/s sustained at bs=1 on T4 (~40 min for 6019 samples), ~83% faster than VoxelNeXt (1.37 it/s) despite the larger transformer head. Reason: TransFusion-L sparse-encoder voxelisation drops a much larger fraction of background voxels than VoxelNeXt's full-grid sparse pass. This is the second nuScenes data point for the *Detector cost* column of `paper/04_experiments.md §4.5` Table 3.

## 8. Issues encountered & resolutions

- Same NumPy / `nuscenes-devkit==1.0.5` incompatibility as `E1.3 §8`; resolved by the shared shim at the top of `pcdet/datasets/nuscenes/nuscenes_dataset.py`.
- **No new issues for the trainval run.** The libnvvm-free env carried over without modification; spconv produced the standard `torch.cuda.amp.custom_fwd/bwd` deprecation FutureWarning but no functional impact. The `epoch_no_number` artefact-directory name is the OpenPCDet default when the checkpoint dict has no `epoch` key (release artifact); not an error.
