# E7.1 — Score-net Loss Upgrade · KITTI E2.2 Perturbation

## 1. Purpose

Test whether improved local score-net training losses can strengthen ASAP beyond the current `tstar008` setting.

Current baseline:

- Checkpoint: `checkpoints/kitti/asap_score_net_trackA_v1.pth`
- Inference: `score_anchor_blend=0.75`, `t_star=0.08`
- KITTI PointPillars E2.2: **61.26 / 34.76**
- KITTI PV-RCNN E2.2: **11.23 / 6.26**

## 2. Linked paper section

Potential future update to `paper/03_method.md` M3 and `paper/04_experiments.md` ablations. Do not update paper-facing claims until a loss variant passes both detector gates.

## 3. Setup

- Dataset: KITTI train clean patches for training.
- Attack for validation: KITTI E2.2 Perturbation.
- First detector gate: PointPillars.
- Confirmation detector: PV-RCNN.
- Runner requirements: all long jobs in `tmux`; use both T4 GPUs where possible.

## 8. Analysis

L1 validates the loss-upgrade direction. Time-balanced DSM does not change M1/M2 selection or point-count behavior: the full E2.2 purified split has the same **27.78%** flagged SPU ratio, **23.71%** score-net SPU ratio, and **21.93%** edited-point ratio as L0. Therefore, metric differences come from the trained score field rather than from changed purification coverage.

The result is not yet strong enough to rewrite the paper's main method claim. PointPillars is effectively flat: Car improves from **61.2567** to **61.3504**, while mAP moves from **34.7557** to **34.7457**. PV-RCNN is more encouraging: Car is flat, but mAP improves from **6.2564** to **6.4569**, mostly through Cyclist AP. This is exactly the type of cross-class recovery that the previous `t_star=0.10` sweep failed to provide.

The remaining weakness is class balance. L1 improves PV-RCNN Cyclist but slightly lowers PV-RCNN Pedestrian, and PointPillars Cyclist drops while Pedestrian improves. L2 tested whether explicit geometry reconstruction could protect local shape, but the Chamfer-heavy auxiliary term reduced PointPillars mAP from **34.7457** to **34.5159**. L3 then removed Chamfer and kept only kNN spacing preservation, but still reduced mAP to **34.3561**. Therefore, auxiliary reconstruction/spacing losses are not the right immediate path: they preserve training-side geometry proxies but degrade detector-useful geometry after purification.

## 9. Decision

Promote L1 as the current E7 candidate, but do not update the paper main table yet. The improvement is real enough to justify the loss branch, but too small to claim a new method contribution by itself.

Next step: `L1b_anchor085` shows that increasing anchor blend to **0.85** is too conservative: it slightly helps Cyclist but heavily hurts Car and Pedestrian. The next candidate is `L1c_step07`: keep `score_anchor_blend=0.75` and `t_star=0.08`, but reduce `score_step_size` from **1.0** to **0.7** to shrink score displacement without fully pulling points back to the anchor.

Gate `L1c_step07` against L1:

- PointPillars mAP should exceed **34.7457**, or at minimum recover sparse-class AP while keeping mAP within `-0.05`.
- If PointPillars passes, run PV-RCNN confirmation and compare against **6.4569** mAP.
- If it fails, document the inference-side negative result and move to a different mechanism rather than more loss/step-size sweeps.
